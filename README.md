<h1>Soft Witch Deck</h1>

<p>
Soft Witch Deck is an interactive mobile experience that combines
mindfulness, journaling, botanical knowledge and magical aesthetics
into a calm digital sanctuary inspired by cottagecore, herbal folklore
and gentle witchcraft.
</p>

</section>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>
Designed as a peaceful daily companion, the application encourages
users to slow down, reflect and build meaningful rituals through
beautiful illustrations, soft interactions and immersive transitions.
Rather than focusing on fantasy roleplay, the experience embraces
wellness, intentional living and self-care.
</p>

<h2>Visual Style</h2>

<p>
The interface is heavily inspired by cottagecore,
vintage botanical books,
moon journals,
handwritten grimoires
and soft fantasy aesthetics.
Classic serif typography is paired with clean modern body text to create
an experience that feels warm,
calm and timeless.
</p>

<h2>Design Goals</h2>

<p>
The project focuses on creating a relaxing digital environment where
technology feels slow, intentional and comforting. Every interaction,
animation and illustration has been designed to reinforce mindfulness
instead of productivity, encouraging users to reconnect with nature,
rituals and themselves.
</p>
